# Website user guide

- Create a campaign or accept an invitation from another member. The creator is its Owner and a GM; ownership remains separate from GM membership. Invite existing users from the Members view and manage roles and presentation in the campaign.
- Create a blank sheet in Characters or Creatures, or generate and inspect a complete preview before saving it. A new sheet is private. Owners can give named users viewer/editor access and request assignments to multiple campaigns; a destination GM approves each request. A campaign link or ID also permits a request when the entity owner is not a campaign member; it does not grant membership. Campaign visibility is chosen independently for each assignment.
- Open a sheet from a campaign to announce eligible combat events there. Opening it from the library keeps combat personal. Start an exclusive editing session before modifying fields or using combat actions. Choosing a level saves it immediately. Other edits save together when you leave a section or choose Save. Undo/Redo is a preview until explicitly saved, and can be cancelled. A disconnected or inactive editor loses the lock without an automatic draft save.
- A campaign member may select an eligible assigned character as their Playing Character. Its limited member preview is visible to campaign members even when the complete sheet is private.
- The personal Dice page is private. Save a texture and glow there to use them across sessions and campaigns. Campaign rolls show the roller’s appearance and can be public or private to the roller and GMs. The D2, D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20 and D100 animate before their results appear, including rolls with multiple dice and modifiers. Read the D4 at its tip. The percentile dice show tens on the left and units on the right, with 00 and 0 representing 100. A natural D20 roll of 1 is a critical success and 20 is a critical failure. Both have a five-second reveal. Reduced motion softens critical effects and shows ordinary results immediately. Chat disappears on server restart or five minutes after the last member leaves that campaign workspace.
- Portraits and campaign covers accept PNG, JPEG, or WebP, at most 4 MiB and 32 million pixels, with no dimension exceeding 8192 pixels. Images are stored in SQLite; portrait history is deduplicated per entity.

The deployed text retains the language used when it was generated; changing the interface language does not translate saved text. For account provisioning see the [README](../README.md). The [French guide](user-guide-fr.md) covers the same principal workflows.
